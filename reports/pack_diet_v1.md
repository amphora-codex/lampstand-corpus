# LampStand corpus — pack diet v1 (integer ids, posting blobs, int8 vectors)

**CANDIDATE MEASUREMENT — corpus half only.** The app-side pack-resolution migration happens in the app repo against the contract in `docs/pack-diet.md`.

Corpus reference: `corpus-v2.0.0-candidate`.

## 1. Why (verified accounting of the v1 pack)

`ondemand_embeddings.sqlite` (v1) was 1,990,508,544 B. Measured against the built DB:

- BM25 postings: 10,831,300 rows, each repeating a 28-char TEXT chunk id — **289 MB of id strings per copy**, stored ~3× (table row + `(term_id, chunk_id)` PK index + `idx_posting_chunk`) plus per-row b-tree overhead ⇒ ~1.6 GB for ~tens of MB of information (df/tf/gaps).
- Dense vectors: 257 MB float32 (175,442 × 384 × 4 B).
- Chunk display text: 102 MB.

v2 fixes the representation: stable **integer** chunk ids, one varint-delta posting BLOB per term, `bm25_doc` folded into `chunk.doc_len`, vectors split into their own pack and stored int8 with a per-vector scale (float32 available behind the `package fp32` flag).
